# Track 021 specification — External governance and partnership activation

## Objective

Activate external governance, country-node, partnership and representation
claims only when attributable, scope-matched evidence exists.

## Requirements

- require a new repository-owner decision for an exact activation candidate;
- record constituted patient/community, scientific, ethics and node authority;
- preserve consent, representation, conflicts, withdrawal and remuneration terms;
- record jurisdiction-specific CARE and Indigenous Data Sovereignty dispositions;
- activate country nodes only after agreement and accreditation;
- confirm relationships, endorsements, access or funding only from written evidence;
- permit global claims only after the roadmap representation conditions pass.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Every activated authority has an attributable remit and withdrawal route.
2. Every country node has a jurisdiction-specific agreement and accreditation.
3. Every confirmed relationship has written counterparty evidence.
4. Custodian, publisher and licensor conditions are recorded without inference.
5. Global claims have represented-jurisdiction and underserved-population evidence.
6. An exact-candidate review records conflicts, dissent, residual risk and owner disposition.

## Non-functional constraints

- Keep sensitive relationship material outside public Git when required.
- Expose only the minimum public receipt needed to substantiate a claim.
- Fail closed on expired, withdrawn, ambiguous or scope-mismatched evidence.

## External gates

All acceptance criteria depend on evidence from the relevant external authority
or counterparty. Repository-owner or agent statements cannot substitute for it.

## Entry conditions

- Track 017 is complete;
- an exact candidate identifies which standing-register conditions apply;
- the repository owner authorises that bounded candidate to enter implementation.

## Non-goals

- bounded single-owner repository governance completed by Track 015;
- speculative activation without an exact candidate decision;
- automatic outreach or implied consent;
- activation of controlled data without custodian approval.
