# Track 013 specification — Quality, validation, gap mapping and equity assurance

## Objective

Create the assurance layer that grades evidence, validates estimates, exposes public-data gaps and prevents unqualified global or comparative claims.

## Required outputs

- evidence-quality, bias and transportability framework;
- machine-readable public-data gap map by parameter, geography, population and disease group;
- internal, external, predictive and face-validity procedures;
- calibration and triangulation reports for demonstrators;
- uncertainty decomposition and model-criticism outputs;
- equity assessment covering diagnostic capacity, underserved populations and distributional consequences;
- LMIC and Indigenous data-governance review criteria;
- GATHER-aligned reporting checklist;
- release language rules based on evidence maturity;
- independent assurance report.

## Acceptance criteria

1. M-26 is satisfied with both machine-readable and human-readable gap products.
2. Quality grades cite evidence and do not collapse multiple dimensions into an opaque score.
3. At least one released analysis is independently reproduced.
4. Demonstrator estimates are triangulated against independent evidence where available.
5. Global and country-comparison language changes according to coverage and uncertainty.
6. Equity analysis identifies who is absent, who may be harmed and which data investments would reduce bias.
7. Applicable reporting checklist items are complete.
8. Blocking assurance findings prevent atlas release.

## Non-goals

- a league table of data quality detached from context;
- hiding missingness through imputation solely for visual completeness;
- treating expert consensus as independent validation;
- claiming global representativeness without diverse leadership and evidence.

## v1 contribution

This track supplies the scientific and equity assurance required for V1-SCI-06 to V1-SCI-09 and V1-GOV-05 to V1-GOV-07.
