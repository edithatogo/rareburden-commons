## Bounded quality and model criticism

The synthetic chain can test structural properties only: release binding,
semantic scope, parameter activation, missingness and suppression preservation,
uncertainty visibility and claim maturity. Calibration thresholds, predictive
validity and triangulation against real evidence are not assessable from these
fixtures. A changed hash, activated empirical flag, unsuppressed protected row,
or upgraded representativeness/equity claim is a blocking failure.
