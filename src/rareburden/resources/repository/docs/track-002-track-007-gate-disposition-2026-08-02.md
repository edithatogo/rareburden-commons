# Track 002 / Track 007 dependency disposition

**Status:** panel recommendation; the Track 007 external challenge gate remains
open and Track 002 remains `in_review`.

## Recommended handling

Keep Track 007 as a narrow release gate. Repository-owned preparation may
freeze the versioned search protocol, rerun bounded searches, normalize and
deduplicate records, preserve exclusions and counts, and map claims to sources.
Novelty and partnership language must remain provisional and must not be used
to close Track 002 or release v0.3.0.

## Options

- **A (recommended):** complete the versioned protocol/search packet, preserve
  raw metadata and exclusions, then seek the required independent challenge and
  patient/community interpretation receipts.
- **B (safe fallback):** publish only a manifest/methods or synthetic preview;
  remove novelty, completeness, partnership, endorsement and coverage claims.
- **C (scope reduction):** drop landscape-derived claims entirely and retain a
  named-source inventory. Record this as a formal downstream scope decision.

## Hard stops

- Do not call an unregistered protocol a completed systematic/scoping review.
- Do not infer novelty, completeness, partnership, endorsement or community
  acceptability from API totals, a seed catalogue, or a subagent panel.
- Do not resolve uncertain/excluded records without the specified adjudication
  evidence.
- Do not release Track 002 v0.3.0 while the dependency remains unresolved.

The panel can prepare and challenge the evidence packet, but cannot provide the
independent methods or patient/community authority required by the gate.
