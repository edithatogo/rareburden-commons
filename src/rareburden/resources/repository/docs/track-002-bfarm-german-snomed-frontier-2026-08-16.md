# Track 002 BfArM German SNOMED CT frontier — 2026-08-16

## Bounded official observation

BfArM describes the National Edition Germany as the International Edition plus
the German National Extension. The extension can contain German translations,
Germany-specific concepts, mappings and subsets. Native packages are RF2 and
are scheduled twice yearly, on 15 May and 15 November, through the authenticated
MLDS download centre. The public BfArM surfaces record release events on
2024-05-15, 2025-05-23 and 2025-11-21; those observations do not expose exact
package names, bytes or checksums and do not prove the complete release history.

BfArM also states that there is no quality-assured complete German translation.
German translation work is use-case based. The archive must not label the
edition as a complete German translation.

## Rights and routing

An affiliate licence is required. BfArM states that the member-funded licence
is free for use in Germany, is limited to German territory and requires an
annual use declaration. The official affiliate agreement permits specified
internal, information-system and research uses of the International Release,
but requires an agreement with the relevant member for its National Release or
Extension. The observed public terms do not affirm that German-extension bytes
may be stored in the owner's private Hugging Face repository from the current
territory or storage location.

Accordingly, public repositories may retain only official URLs, factual release
event metadata, checksums obtained from the publisher, manifests and receipts.
Raw public redistribution is disabled. Private raw archival is also disabled
until an authenticated MLDS inventory records the exact package and agreement,
the applicable territory is authorized, and the agreement or member decision
affirmatively permits the proposed private-cloud storage. Public availability,
an owner statement or an International Release licence cannot satisfy these
conditions for the German National Extension.

## Duplication and gap disposition

The bounded UTS inventory contains 86 International, 30 Spanish, 19 US, 59 core
problem-list subset, 26 ICD-10-CM map and 13 US transitive-closure releases. It
contains no German, Germany or Deutsch-labelled release. The German National
Extension and native German Edition therefore remain a real inventory gap.
Because a National Edition bundles an International Edition, an authorized
future package must be checked by exact SHA-256 and existing private remote path
before upload; only the non-duplicate exact artifact may enter the existing
private archive.

or uploaded:

1. authenticated MLDS product, edition, effective date, filename and checksum;
2. exact accepted affiliate and German member/national agreement identifiers;
3. affirmative authorization for the operator's territory and intended use;
4. affirmative authorization for the proposed private-cloud storage location;
5. required attribution, reporting, withdrawal and end-user conditions;
6. exact-hash comparison with existing UTS/MLDS private receipts; and
7. a reviewed inventory row accepted by the existing bounded licensed-portal
   workflow.

Absent any one condition, retain metadata only. No portal login, release-file
download, byte upload or completeness claim was performed in this slice.
