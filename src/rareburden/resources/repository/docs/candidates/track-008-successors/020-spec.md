# Candidate specification — 020 clinical, community and semantic assurance

## Objective

Hold the attributable evidence gates that repository-owned simulation and
metadata cannot satisfy for real-source, empirical, public-facing, clinical,
patient-facing or authority-bearing semantic use.

## Required outputs

- exact derivative-publication and field-level third-party rights dispositions;
- attributable clinical and semantic-methods mapping-fitness review;
- attributable patient/community review of naming and aggregation harms;
- attributable independent semantic and engineering review where claimed;
- exact decisions for ORDO/Orphadata, ICD-10, ICD-11, OMIM, extended HPO,
  MedDRA/MLDS and any other selected terminology;
- candidate-bound public rendering, correction, withdrawal and monitoring plans.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Every included artifact has exact rights, provenance, transformation and route evidence.
2. Clinical and mapping-fitness claims are bound to attributable methods and reviewers.
3. Naming and aggregation findings are bound to attributable community evidence.
4. Public-facing renderers preserve uncertainty, provisional status and limitations.
5. Correction and withdrawal procedures are tested without claiming retraction of Git history.
6. No simulated panel, owner decision or metadata is treated as external authority.

## Non-goals

- manufacturing consent, representation, rights or approval through agent simulation;
- treating source metadata, licences labels or mappings as fitness evidence;
- authorizing controlled terminology without exact credentials and edition terms.
