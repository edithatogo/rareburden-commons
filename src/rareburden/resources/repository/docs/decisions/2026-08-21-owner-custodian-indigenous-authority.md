# Owner custodian and Indigenous-authority declaration

**Date:** 2026-08-21  
**Decision maker:** `edithatogo`, repository owner

## Decision

The repository owner declares that they are the custodian for this repository
and its owner-controlled data pathways, and that they hold the applicable
Indigenous-authority role for this work. These are accountable owner-held roles,
not advisory-agent roles and not independent review.

No additional or independent human reviewer is required or planned. Review is
performed by role-separated advisory agent panels, and the owner makes every
decision.

## Scope limits

This declaration does not claim authority for unrelated Indigenous peoples or
communities, third-party custodians, publishers, licensors, ethics bodies,
governments or jurisdictions. If future work invokes one of those authorities,
the corresponding evidence remains separately required.

## Continuing controls

- All repository work remains free; remuneration is zero.
- Publisher licences and third-party rights remain binding facts.
- Agent advice cannot be described as human consultation or independent review.
- Decisions remain attributable to the owner and bound to the relevant scope.
