# Track 002 bounded analytical activation

The repository owner activates two exact analytical rows only:

- the exact July 2026 Orphadata files for identifier alignment and descriptive
  metadata under CC BY 4.0, with attribution, change notices and no endorsement;
- the exact WPP 2024 workbook for Australia and New Zealand, 2000–2021,
  medium-variant national total population denominators only, under CC BY 3.0
  IGO with attribution.

This is an analytical-use decision, not authorization to broaden the minimal
public byte package. Orphadata epidemiology cannot support prevalence or burden
inference. WPP cannot be interpolated or substituted across age, sex, geography,
year or variant. WHO remains candidate/private-only pending field-level
third-party and modification rights. World Bank remains probe/cross-check-only
and cannot substitute for WPP.

Any exact release, hash, licence, attribution, estimand, geography, year,
variant or semantic-scope drift stops the affected row and restores the
metadata/hash-only posture pending a new decision.
