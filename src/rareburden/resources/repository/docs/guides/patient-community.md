# Patient and community user guide

RareBurden Commons is intended to support transparent burden research. The
current repository is a pre-release technical handoff: it does not publish an
empirical burden estimate, claim community endorsement, or provide clinical
advice.

## Acceptable use

Use outputs to inspect methods, assumptions, uncertainty and aggregate public
evidence. Do not use synthetic examples to describe the lived experience or
prevalence of a community. Do not infer individual risk, eligibility or service
need from an aggregate result.

## Harm and correction

Report a harmful framing, disclosure concern, suspected error or inaccessible
material through the repository issue process without including personal or
controlled data. Preserve the affected version and quote only public artefact
identifiers. Publication and scope decisions require patient/community and
other accountable review; a maintainer cannot substitute for that review.
