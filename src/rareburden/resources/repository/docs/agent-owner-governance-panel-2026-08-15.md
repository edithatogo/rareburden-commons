# Agent-panel review: single-developer governance transition

**Date:** 2026-08-15  
**Disposition:** recommend accept with fail-closed rights and continuity limits  
**Owner decision:** accepted through ADR-0009

## Panel perspectives

| Perspective | Finding | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Review-contract audit | Active plans mixed agent preparation with mandatory independent or additional-person review gates | Replace future repository review gates with role-separated agent advice and owner disposition; preserve historical provenance |
| Public-source rights audit | Track 002 used `custodian` for four public sources rather than controlled patient data | Separate owner data-use decisions from immutable publisher licences and third-party rights; reserve custodian policy for future controlled deployment |
| Continuity audit | The owner reports backup-role acceptance, but public evidence does not yet record scope, escalation, expiry or a handoff exercise | Record privacy-preserving owner attestation now; keep continuity exercise in progress until its operational fields are evidenced |

No panel dissent was recorded. The panel does not claim independence, human or
community participation, licensor authority, institutional authority or a
completed backup handoff.

## Stop conditions

Critical safety, rights, semantic-integrity, security, reproducibility,
recovery, withdrawal or hash failure requires narrowing, revision or stop.
