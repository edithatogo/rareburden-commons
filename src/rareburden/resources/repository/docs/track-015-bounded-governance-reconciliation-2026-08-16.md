# Track 015 bounded governance reconciliation — 2026-08-16

## Current operating model

RareBurden Commons is a single-developer repository. The repository owner is
accountable for exact-candidate scope, corrections, withdrawal and release
decisions. Role-separated agent panels provide methods, rights/data-use,
community-harm/accessibility, engineering/security/reproducibility and
operations/release challenge. Their advice is not independent, human, patient,
community, custodian, institutional or external approval.

This bounded model makes repository decisions auditable without claiming that
the future governance bodies in Track 015 have been constituted. The full track
therefore remains planned.

Publisher licences, third-party rights and controlled-environment rules remain

partnership, consent, representation, registry events or custodian authority.
Uncertain rights remain metadata/hash-only, and archive presence never activates
an estimand, clinical claim or production route.

## Relationships and claims

IHME, WHO, RDI, WEF, genomic programmes and funders remain proposed with no
contact, endorsement or access claim. Orphanet/Orphadata is recorded only as a
public-source relationship under exact publisher terms, not as a partnership.
Any future `confirmed` state requires attributable written evidence and a new
owner disposition; repository prose alone cannot create it.

Constituted governance, patient/community or Indigenous authority, custodian
approval, scientific independence, institutional hosting, partnership, global
representativeness, production activation and stable release remain false.

## Correction and withdrawal

Rights or terms changes, withdrawal notices, newly discovered third-party
material, hash/provenance mismatch, critical safety/semantic/security findings,
reproduction/recovery failure, unsupported relationship claims and material
scope inflation stop the affected route. Freeze it, preserve redacted evidence
and history, mark the artifact withdrawn, superseded or disabled, and require a
new panel challenge plus owner disposition for any replacement. External
notification occurs only through an authorized relationship route.

## Remaining Track 015 boundary

Repository controls do not satisfy the specification's future constituted
governance, remuneration, country-node/Indigenous authority, confirmed
partnership, policy-scope or tabletop/readiness acceptance criteria. Those
remain visibly pending rather than being relabelled as agent review.
