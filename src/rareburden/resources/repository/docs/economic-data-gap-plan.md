# Patient and family economic data gap and collection plan

## 1. Current evidence gaps

1. **Unpaid caregiver time valuation:** Significant heterogeneity in wage replacement rates vs opportunity cost approaches across jurisdictions.
2. **Out-of-pocket travel burdens:** Fragmented records regarding indirect travel costs for rural and remote rare disease families accessing tertiary centres.
3. **Adult rare disease transitions:** Paucity of longitudinal data capturing lost employment and superannuation/pension deficits among adults with paediatric-onset rare diseases.
4. **Nonmonetary quality-of-life exclusions:** Economic evaluations frequently exclude social participation restriction and sibling impacts.

## 2. New collection plan

1. **Phase 1: Multi-centre registry linkage (administrative):** Focus on linking disease-specific clinical registry cohorts with national health insurance and hospital statistics.
2. **Phase 2: Targeted survey modules (patient/family):** Administer RBC-P001D survey core through accredited patient advocacy organisations once ethics approvals and participant compensation funding are in place. The required co-design, accessibility and compensation protocol is documented in [track-005-codesign-protocol-2026-09-06.md](track-005-codesign-protocol-2026-09-06.md); it remains a plan and does not authorize collection.
3. **Phase 3: Economic parameter ledger integration:** Normalise observed costs and hours into versioned parameter fixtures under Track 009 schema contracts with explicit quality and transportability flags. A schema-conformant, data-free scaffold is provided at [examples/economics/economic-ledger-template.yml](../examples/economics/economic-ledger-template.yml); it must be completed only with real sourced observations, and richer quality/transportability flag fields require a schema contract revision with owner disposition.
