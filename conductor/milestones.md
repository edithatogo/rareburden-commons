## Milestone rules

- Milestones are gate-based, not calendar promises.
- A milestone is complete only when all assigned tracks are Complete and its roadmap exit gates pass.
- A later milestone may begin exploratory work, but cannot use an incomplete dependency as release evidence.
- Scientific validity, participant safety, data rights, patient/community authority and critical security findings cannot be waived to advance a milestone.
- A release may narrow its supported scope when evidence is insufficient; the product promise and acceptance evidence must change with it.
