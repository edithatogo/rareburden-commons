# Community conduct

Participation must be respectful, inclusive and safe for people living with rare diseases, families, clinicians, researchers, data custodians and contributors from diverse regions and disciplines.

Expected behaviour includes listening to lived experience, declaring conflicts, criticising methods rather than people, respecting cultural and jurisdictional differences, protecting confidentiality and sharing credit fairly.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, misuse of sensitive information, tokenistic patient involvement and attempts to bypass data governance are unacceptable.

A formal enforcement and confidential-reporting process will be adopted with the project's institutional host and patient/community governance. Until then, concerns should be raised privately with the founding maintainer or host rather than through public disclosure.
