# Track 014 accessibility and disclosure checklist

**Status:** repository preparation; no public API or beta publication enabled.

Every static, package and API-shaped aggregate projection must:

- use a meaningful heading and descriptive link text;
- expose missingness as `not_assessed` or another explicit non-zero state;
- expose uncertainty, limitations and scope exclusions in text;
- preserve the release and source-manifest identity;
- remain aggregate-only and read-only;
- avoid colour-only, hover-only or visual-only meaning;
- remain usable from the documented offline workflow.

This checklist is an engineering aid, not an accessibility or community
acceptance decision or accessibility certification. Under ADR-0009, advisory
accessibility/usability challenge and repository-owner disposition remain pending
for the exact candidate. This wording change does not satisfy either gate or
demonstrate rendered-interface, keyboard or assistive-technology conformance.

Owner-executed simulated-community challenge; no actual community participation,
representation, consultation, endorsement, consent or independent review.
Patient/community needs and real-user usability are not established by this
checklist. Applicable third-party rights and publication decisions remain separate.
