## Recommended implementation policy

Implement the full reference-methods and software scope, not another validation-only
prototype: typed cost/time/productivity/education/social contracts; declared
perspectives; component calculations; auditable conversion and discounting;
scenario propagation; distributional reporting; monogenic-diabetes reference
integration; paediatric input specifications; and survey/data-gap documentation.

Require explicit caller-supplied currency, price year, rate direction/source,
discount rate/convention, quantity denominator, overlap assessment and valuation
assumptions. Do not choose empirical prices, a universal caregiver wage, a PPP
series or a discount rate silently. Retain resource quantities beside monetary
valuations. Treat nonvalued care and missing education/participation outcomes as
visible gaps, not zero costs.

Keep transfer cash flows visible by payer/recipient and perspective; exclude
within-society transfers from resource-cost totals, with the societal boundary
declared. Prevent combining incompatible contexts and overlapping valuation
methods. Label invented examples as scenarios, never observed economic burden.
Reference integration does not activate patient data or publish empirical results.
