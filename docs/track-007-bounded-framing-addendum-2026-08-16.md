# Track 007 bounded framing addendum

**Purpose:** Apply the community/harm advisory challenge to interpretation of
the frozen Track 007 evidence without rewriting the v0.2.0 protocol or implying
patient, community, institutional or external approval.

**Status:** Required framing for any owner disposition or public use of the
bounded evidence synthesis.

The v0.2.0 protocol was frozen after the 2026-08-14 and 2026-08-15 searches and
screens. It is therefore a `retrospective_repository_protocol`: it makes the
completed repository workflow reconstructable but is not preregistration and
must not be described as prospective or confirmatory.

## Plain-language boundary

RareBurden Commons sampled a limited set of indexed, predominantly
English-language public records. Within that sample, existing organisations and
infrastructures already provide important registries, terminology, genomic,
policy and burden-related functions. The sample suggests a possible
complementary role for provenance-aware, federated measurement and policy
translation. This is an unresolved design opportunity within the observed
sources. It does not prove that this role is globally novel or that other work
is absent.

The search is incomplete. It may miss non-English, regional, community-led,
small-group, restricted, unindexed and grey-literature work. Sixty-nine records
still need lawful content assessment, and a separate 306-occurrence live
capture has not been screened or reconciled. No patient or community body has
approved the framing. Agent advice is not patient/community consent or
constituted-community legitimacy.

## Non-valuation and acceptable-use statement

System-level burden measurement does not measure the worth of a person, family,
community or condition. It must not be used to rank whose life matters, justify
withholding care, ration services mechanically or erase lived experience.
Interpretation must expose uncertainty, missingness, equity consequences and
the limits of the sampled evidence.

## Catalogue interpretation

The 13 institutional records in `catalog/initiatives.yml` are owner-curated
examples used to test adjacency fields. They are not a global inventory and do
not establish geographic representativeness. Fields such as
`candidate_partner`, `recommended_engagement`, `use` and `remaining_gap` record
owner-considered complementarity or scope boundaries only. They do not evidence
a relationship, invitation, endorsement, consent, access or authority over the
named organisation or community.

A record without accessible evidence is `not_assessable`; it is never treated
as absent, irrelevant or inferior. Missing community authority, self-described
language and geography, intended benefit, accessibility, and Indigenous or
cultural-governance information remain unknown. A later protocol should add
those fields explicitly rather than infer them.

## Accessibility and release requirements

Any public landscape output must:

- include the plain-language boundary and non-valuation statement above;
- identify the exact sampled sources, dates, queries and page budgets;
- distinguish curated seeds, screened snapshots and unscreened captures;
- provide accessible text for visual comparisons;
- avoid colour-only meaning and unexplained technical labels;
- label translation and non-English discovery as unresolved work; and
- provide a correction route for misdescription, omission or harmful framing.

## Stop triggers

Stop or withdraw an affected claim if it:

- values or ranks people, communities or conditions or is used to justify
  mechanical rationing;
- infers absence, irrelevance or inferiority from unobserved, inaccessible or
  unindexed evidence;
- implies partnership, consent, community approval, endorsement or access;
- presents the sample as comprehensive, globally representative or confirmed
  novelty; or
- leaves a material naming, rights, accessibility or cultural-governance harm
  unresolved.
