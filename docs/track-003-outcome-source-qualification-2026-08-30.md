## Source observations

### Steele 2014: complication candidate remains held

The [publisher article](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/1814212)
for DOI `10.1001/jama.2013.283980` was readable through the research browser on
30 August 2026. The publisher dates publication to 15 January 2014, resolving
the first tranche's month-only date. No article bytes were archived or hashed;
this is a located observation, not an exact-byte source receipt.

Methods describes bilateral retinal imaging with blinded grading. Results
identifies an any-retinopathy endpoint with its own assessed denominator;
the overall recruited population is not that denominator. A non-sparse
endpoint is a candidate for later extraction, not an activated parameter.
The selected adult GCK cross-section cannot establish incidence, lifetime
risk, causal protection or outcomes for all monogenic diabetes.

No unrestricted article redistribution licence was verified. The publisher
offers a permissions route. Public readability is not permission to archive
article text, tables or supplements. Hold quantitative promotion pending an
exact intended-use rights disposition, endpoint/missingness verification and
disclosure review. Sparse and complementary subgroup cells remain excluded.

### Pang 2022: referral-pathway candidate remains held

`10.2337/dc21-2056` was inspected on 30 August 2026. One transient HTTP 200

Bytes were not retained; subsequent page responses may differ. Europe PMC's
fullTextXML endpoint returned 404; PMC HTML provided a bounded fallback.

The article's terms restrict use to attributed, educational, non-profit,
unaltered use. They are not an unrestricted open licence or a grant to
relicense the article under repository terms. Bare factual extraction and
article redistribution are distinct; the exact public-use basis remains
unresolved rather than presumed prohibited or permitted.

The printed GDN percentage does not agree with its accompanying numerator
and denominator. Do not silently repair it or promote that result. The
abstract and Methods also differ on whether the lower diagnosis-age boundary
is exclusive or inclusive. Neither discrepancy is resolved here.

The calculator analysis counts diagnostic request forms during August 2014
through December 2019, restricted to the source-described White, diagnosis-age
1–35 subset. This reporting information was unavailable for referrals to the
Scottish laboratory in this study. These are historical model and capture
restrictions, not biological eligibility or clinical advice.
Forms are not demonstrated unique people, healthcare encounters or person-time.

## Disposition and next evidence

The source observations narrow the blockers but do not clear them. No new
quantitative record, model parameter, comparison or source archive is created.
Empirical execution, controlled-data use, synthesis, independent review,
community representation, publication and production-release authority remain
false for this addendum. Existing owner authorization does not supply missing
third-party rights or the exact evidence required by
[issue 261](https://github.com/edithatogo/rareburden-commons/issues/261).

Recommended next actions, in order:

1. Preserve both holds and the original first-tranche receipt. Complete an
   exact intended-use rights assessment before publishing extracted aggregates
   from either source. Do not treat a rights assessment as publisher permission.
2. For Steele, verify the endpoint-specific assessed population, missingness,
   family dependence, severity definition and disclosure-safe extraction.
   Retain prevalence semantics; do not use a case fraction to allocate outcomes.
3. For Pang, use a source-consistent referral-form result only after resolving
   the counting unit, duplicate/family handling and intended-use basis. Keep
   the GDN arithmetic discrepancy and age-boundary discrepancy explicit.
4. In parallel source discovery, qualify an open-licensed alternative without
   silently changing the target population or substituting referral yield for
   utilization. General diabetes or mitochondrial-disease utilization is not
   automatically monogenic-diabetes evidence.

- Recommended: retain holds and pursue a source-compatible open alternative.
  This keeps public reuse conservative but may leave the evidence gap open.
- Alternative: prepare a narrowly scoped intended-use or permission request
  for the existing studies if essential. This may preserve source continuity
  but requires external clarification; no request has been sent.

The next repository-owned qualification work needs no new user decision.
External contact, changed scientific scope or a governed exact-candidate
disposition must be presented separately. Stop promotion on ambiguous rights,
unresolved conflicting numbers, inferred denominators, sparse disclosure,
unjustified transport or a causal/clinical reinterpretation.
