# HPO v2026-06-23 private preservation notice

The private source archive preserves seven core ontology and annotation
artifacts from Human Phenotype Ontology release `v2026-06-23`. Each local
SHA-256 matches the digest published with the GitHub release.

The exact files embed `https://hpo.jax.org/app/license` as their licence route,
but that route did not yield an accessible terms document during the recorded
observation. The files therefore remain private pending capture of the exact
terms. HPO and its contributors must be acknowledged, annotation provenance
must be retained, and the files must not be treated as direct diagnostic
evidence or as establishing clinical validity or comprehensive coverage.
