# Track 007 bounded technical-scope disposition

**Decision:** narrow

The repository owner accepts Track 007 as a bounded technical adjacency and
coverage-observation deliverable. Its evidence may describe only what was
observed within the recorded providers, queries, languages, regions, request
caps and timestamps.

Patient/community authority is not required for that deliberately technical
deliverable because it makes no claim about community acceptability,
legitimacy, consent, partnership, representativeness, global coverage or
confirmed novelty. The community-authority gate is therefore non-applicable to
this scope, not satisfied. Agent advice and the owner decision are not
patient/community approval.

Any future community-facing interpretation, partnership recommendation,
patient-priority claim or assertion of legitimacy requires a separately
versioned scope and attributable evidence. Provider-rights drift, hash failure,
unqualified absence or novelty language, or an implied community/partnership
claim stops publication until narrowed or corrected.

This decision supersedes the earlier Track 007 posture of retaining this exact
bounded deliverable in review solely for community-authority evidence. It does
not erase that historical decision or close the broader evidence gaps recorded
in the Track 007 review.
