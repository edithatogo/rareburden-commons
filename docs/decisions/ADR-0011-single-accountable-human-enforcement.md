# ADR-0011: Enforce one accountable human, owner and maintainer

**Status:** accepted by repository owner — 2026-08-21

## Context

The repository already used a single-owner agent-advice model, but current
track metadata, continuity language and proposed-governance text still implied
multiple internal role-holders, backup owners or constituted bodies. The owner
requested one unambiguous accountable human and asked a role-separated
subagent panel for options before deciding.

## Advisory options

The panel presented three options:

1. a minimal policy declaration, with low migration cost but continuing drift;
2. a canonical policy plus automated repository-wide enforcement;
3. owner approval replacing external and independent evidence gates.

The panel recommended option 2. It rejected option 3 because repository
accountability cannot manufacture rights, consent, clinical validity,
custodian permission, community or Indigenous authority, or independence.

## Owner disposition

The repository owner accepted option 2. `edithatogo` is the sole accountable
repository human, owner, maintainer, developer, approver, scientist, operator,
security decision-maker and release decision-maker.

Agents and bots are non-accountable advisers. External people or institutions
may provide attributable evidence without becoming repository owners or
maintainers. Historical role labels remain provenance and are not rewritten as
current appointments.

Every material advisory packet presents options, trade-offs, contingencies,
rationale and a recommendation, plus uncertainty, dissent and stop triggers.
The owner records the separate decision. Agent advice and owner-operated work
are never independent review.

## Continuity and external boundaries

There is no backup owner or co-maintainer. Recovery procedures and encrypted
recovery material do not confer authority. Owner absence or suspected
credential compromise freezes protected changes, production operations and
releases. A future human role requires an explicit dated governance
transition.

Third-party rights, source terms, custodian rules, controlled-data permission,
clinical validity, patient/community or Indigenous authority, partnership,
endorsement, regulatory requirements and genuinely independent evidence remain
separate facts and fail closed when absent.
