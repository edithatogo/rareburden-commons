# ADR-0009: Agent-panel and repository-owner governance

**Status:** accepted by repository owner — 2026-08-15

## Context

RareBurden Commons is a single-developer repository. Its available review
participants are role-separated agents and the accountable repository owner.
Requiring additional people or describing agent output as independent review
would misstate the operating model.

## Decision

The owner declares that they hold the repository-custodian and applicable
Indigenous-authority roles for the scope recorded in the machine-readable
governance contract. This does not assert authority for unrelated Indigenous
peoples or communities, third-party custodians, publishers or jurisdictions.

All methods, scientific, patient/community-perspective, data-use, engineering,
security, usability, operations and release review is performed as advisory
challenge by a role-separated panel of agents. The panel records evidence,
uncertainty, dissent, stop triggers and an accept, narrow, revise, defer or stop
recommendation. The repository owner records the attributable disposition for
an exact candidate.

Agent-panel evidence is never called independent, human, constituted-community,
institutional, third-party-custodian, licensor or external approval. No
additional or independent human review is planned or required. No additional person
is a mandatory repository review gate.

## Authority and rights boundary

Publisher licences, source terms, third-party rights, registry events and
controlled-data policies are facts that neither agents nor the owner can
manufacture. For public sources, the owner decides repository-controlled scope,
retention, withdrawal, transformation and activation only after exact terms
evidence is recorded. Unresolved rights fail closed. A real controlled-data
deployment must obey the supplying custodian's policy; synthetic/public-only
releases exclude that deployment and need no custodian receipt.

## Operations and release

Owner-operated clean-environment reproduction, security scans, recovery and
rollback exercises are qualifying repository evidence under this operating
model when bound to the exact candidate. The owner is the release authority.
No claim of independence is made.

The owner reports that a privacy-preserving backup-owner role has accepted.
Until its stable private role identifier, scope, escalation route, expiry and
handoff evidence are recorded, that statement is an owner attestation and not
a completed continuity exercise.

## Stop conditions

Critical safety, rights, semantic-integrity, security, reproducibility,
recovery, withdrawal or hash failures require narrowing, revision or stop.
