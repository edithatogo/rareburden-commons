# ADR-0010: Prospective simulated-panel and owner decisions

**Status:** accepted by repository owner — 2026-08-21

## Context

RareBurden Commons is a single-person repository. Future material decisions
need role-separated challenge without inventing additional people, independence
or external authority. Historical responsibility and evidence records remain
provenance and must not be rewritten.

## Decision

Every prospective material repository decision uses the machine-readable
contract in `docs/single-owner-agent-governance.yml`. At least three explicitly
simulated agent perspectives prepare two or three distinct options with
option-specific trade-offs, contingencies, minimum evidence and rationale. The
synthesis preserves uncertainty, dissent and stop triggers, recommends one
option and binds the packet to the exact candidate and evidence manifest. The
repository owner then records the attributable disposition.

A simulated patient/community or harm perspective is not lived-experience
testimony, community representation, consent, legitimacy or endorsement. Agent
simulation cannot create publisher terms, rights, credentials, custodian
permission, partnership or external approval. Missing required facts force a
narrow, revise, defer or stop disposition.

## Consequences

The policy applies prospectively across tracks. Historical role metadata and
accepted evidence bindings are preserved. Materiality determines the relevant
perspectives, but at least three are required for a material-risk decision.
