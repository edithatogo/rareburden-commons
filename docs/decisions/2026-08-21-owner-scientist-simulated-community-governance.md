# Owner-scientist and simulated-community governance disposition — 2026-08-21

**Decision-maker:** repository owner and scientist  
**Disposition:** approve  
**Governance:** ADR-0009 and ADR-0010

## Decision

The repository owner approves the role-separated agent-panel recommendation:

> Agents provide role-separated advisory analysis. The repository owner and
> scientist is the sole accountable decision-maker. Agent reviews and
> owner-operated tests are repository evidence, not independent approval or
> external validation.

The owner also approves this community/harm boundary:

> Owner-executed simulated-community challenge; no actual community
> participation, representation, consultation, endorsement, consent or
> independent review.

This decision is implemented by ADR-0010's prospective simulated-panel and
owner-decision model and applies prospectively to material repository
decisions. Agents must present two or three options with contingencies,
rationale, trade-offs, minimum evidence and recommendations before the owner
records an attributable disposition bound to the exact candidate.

## Operating model

- Use role-separated methods/scientific, clinical, simulated-community/harm,
  rights/data-use, semantic, engineering/reproducibility, security and
  adversarial perspectives in proportion to the decision risk.
- Preserve dissent, absent evidence, uncertainty, conflicts, stop triggers and
  reversal conditions.
- Record the owner disposition as `approve`, `approve_with_conditions`,
  `narrow`, `revise`, `defer` or `stop`.
- Describe owner-operated scientific review, tests and reproduction as
  repository evidence only.
- Describe the public-facing simulated-community lane as a community-impact
  self-assessment where the internal label could imply real participation.

## Simulated-community scope

The simulated-community challenge may test language, accessibility, stigma,
equity, exclusion, aggregation harms, subgroup interpretation, foreseeable
misuse and hypothetical stakeholder perspectives. It may recommend revision,
narrowing or stop.

It cannot confer representation, consent, endorsement, co-design, cultural
safety, social licence, lived-experience evidence or community approval.

## Factual gates retained

Sole-owner authority does not manufacture third-party facts or permissions.
Publisher licences, redistribution rights, source terms, registry events,
controlled-data custodian rules, legally required ethics/privacy approvals,
applicable Indigenous data authority, actual publication and genuine external
replication remain evidence-bound when they enter scope.

If empirical or consequential claims need evidence that the repository does
not possess, the owner must obtain that evidence or narrow, qualify, defer or
stop the claim. Synthetic validation cannot establish empirical validity,
transportability or acceptability to affected people.
